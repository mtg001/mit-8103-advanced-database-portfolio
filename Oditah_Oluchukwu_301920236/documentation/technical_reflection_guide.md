# Individual technical reflection - complete in your own words

Before submission, replace this guide with a genuine reflection based on your own review and test experience. Do not submit the prompts as though they were your personal experience.

Address these points in first person:
1. Why was a relational database selected for account balances, transfers and the ledger?
2. Which entity relationships and integrity constraints mattered most, and why?
3. What changed in the query plan after the composite index was added? What was the measured effect on your machine?
4. What did the commit and rollback tests prove? What concurrency limitation did the SQLite demonstration show?
5. Why did the NoSQL design embed a bounded profile summary but retain transfer events in a separate collection?
6. What trade-off would read replicas introduce for a just-completed transfer?
7. Which part did you personally execute or modify, what problem did you encounter, and how did you verify the result?

Include only challenges and outcomes that actually occurred during your own testing.
